SineVault Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

SineVault is made by one person, Scott Wolff. The short version: the app keeps everything on your device, has no account, no ads and no analytics, and sends nothing anywhere unless you ask it to.

What stays on your device

Your gear list and photos, the sounds you save, your lesson progress, checkpoint scores and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. If you make a backup from the Studio tab, the file goes where you choose to put it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What can leave your device, and only when you choose

  • The AI helper. If you enter your own API key from OpenAI or Anthropic in Studio, then the questions you type, and any gear photo you ask the app to identify, are sent to that provider under your own account and their privacy policy. Without a key, nothing is sent. SineVault does not include a key and does not see your conversations.
  • Tell Scott. The button on the Studio tab opens your Mail app with a message already written: which lesson you were on, your device model and iOS version, the app build, and the last few things you tapped in the app. You can read and edit it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.
  • Sharing a sound. Sharing a SineVault One sound uses the iOS share sheet; it goes only where you send it.

Microphone, camera and photos

The microphone is used only when you record a clip in "Learn a song by ear"; the recording stays on your device. The camera and photo library are used only to add pictures of your own gear. The app asks for each of these the first time you use the feature and never uses them in the background.

MIDI and Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect a MIDI keyboard you choose to pair. No other devices are scanned for and nothing is stored about them.

Ratings

At a few milestones the app may show Apple's standard "rate this app" sheet. That is handled by iOS; the app learns nothing from it.

Children

SineVault is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
